Research

Methods for incomplete biological measurement

The visual pathway is the testbed. The methods — weak labels, missing modalities, irregular time, and the physics of the sensor — are meant to travel.

Living systems are measured through instruments and records that are incomplete, noisy, and physically constrained. My research develops AI and statistical-learning methods that treat those constraints as part of the scientific problem rather than as noise to be ignored.

Last updated from Google Scholar · November 15, 2026. Open Scholar

Independent program

  1. 01

    Learning under incomplete observation

    Living systems are rarely observed with every modality and a clean label. I develop multimodal models that learn shared and view-specific representations, transfer from rich training sets to sparse encounters, score the reliability of weak labels, and abstain or request another measurement when evidence is insufficient. Vision is the first domain; the same structure applies to other imaging and behavioral time series.

  2. 02

    Longitudinal models of living-system change

    Temporal models that respect irregular sampling, quantify uncertainty, and recover heterogeneous trajectories. The target is not a single forecast. It is a representation of how a system evolves, which trajectories are distinct, and when a new observation would change the inference.

  3. 03

    Measurement-aware AI

    Models should not treat OCT, spectroscopy, or functional tests as generic tensors. I couple learning with forward models of how light, tissue, and devices produce the recorded signal, so a prediction stays attached to the measurement that generated it.

Current work

npj Digital Medicine, 2025

Weak supervision from large-scale text

Ci-SSGAN uses unlabeled clinical notes to improve disease annotation relative to ICD codes and to reduce performance gaps across demographic groups. Labels on living systems are often cheap, biased, and incomplete; the model should use abundant unlabeled observation rather than pretend a gold standard exists.

npj Digital Medicine, accepted 2026

Distilled reasoning models

A teacher-distilled language model for clinically aligned rationale generation. The goal is not a larger model, but a smaller one that keeps interpretable structure transferred from a teacher trained on domain text. A clinical note goes in; the demo returns a glaucoma subtype and a one-sentence justification.

Under review, 2026

Sparse longitudinal dynamics

A multi-horizon model for progression from irregular observations. It combines structural images, functional tests, and clinical variables with calibration, uncertainty, and subgroup evaluation. Living systems are temporal; most datasets are not regularly sampled.

Submitted, 2026

Missing views

A cross-modal model trained on paired structure and function that can still infer from imaging alone. That is the usual state of a clinical measurement: not every instrument is used at every visit.

Interpretable functional models

PyGlaucoMetrics extracts a disease signal from noisy visual-field tests. PyVisualFields v2 is an open Python library for standard visual-field analysis, so other groups can reuse the same metrics without an R stack.

Foundations

  • Physics-based optical models

    Forward models of tissue and devices, including collaborative work with the FDA on pulse oximetry and skin pigmentation. An image or a waveform is the output of an optical or physiological process, then learned from with AI.

  • OCT of retinal and kidney tissue

    Ensemble learning for AMD classification from SD-OCT, and soft-attention segmentation of kidney OCT, including robotic large-area imaging for pre-transplant evaluation.

  • Functional brain measurement

    Master’s work on fNIRS signal analysis and a brain-mapping GUI — an early contact with functional measurement, motion artifact, and low signal-to-noise ratio.
